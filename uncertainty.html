Routing under Uncertainty — Literature Audit

Subject: stochastic / robust routing literature with uncertain travel time, energy, demand, or service time.  ·  Audit method: 5-paper schema-derivation read + 45-paper bulk feature-signature extraction; 24 axes across 8 clusters.  ·  ← back to index
Bottom line. The audit covers 50 papers. The most cited method is the budget-polytope adaptive robust model. Most papers compare against a deterministic baseline, but only 24% compare against a different uncertainty method, and only 6% test how the result changes with the number of scenarios.

Spotlight works

PaperMethodological position
Pelletier, Jabali, Laporte 2019 — EVRP with energy-consumption uncertainty (Transp. Res. Part B) [Pelletier2019] The simplest case: a static robust model. The plan is fixed before the route starts. Tests five different ways to describe uncertainty (box, budget, route-dependent budget, ball-box, ellipsoidal). Easy to solve but cautious.
Liu et al. 2025 — joint travel-time + energy uncertainty (Transp. Res. Part E) [TRE2025] An adaptive robust model with two uncertainty sources at once (travel time and energy). The plan can react to what actually happens during the route. Solver: a Bi-Level Search Algorithm combining ALNS with labelling.
Bian et al. 2024 — adaptive robust EVRPTW with partial recharge (Transp. Res. Part C) [BianTRC2024] Adaptive robust with energy uncertainty. The plan can react during the route. Solver: column-and-constraint generation with VNS-TS as the master heuristic.
Han, Lee, Park 2018 — robust VRPTW with hard time windows under demand and travel-time uncertainty (Comput. Oper. Res.) [HanCOR2018] The same adjustable robust method, applied to delivery routing without the electric-vehicle features. Solver: two-stage AVNS — first minimise vehicle count, then minimise distance.
Marinaki & Marinakis 2020 — discrete-scenario worst-case robust VRPTW (Comput. Ind. Eng.) [MarinakiCIE2020] Uses a fixed list of scenarios instead of an uncertainty set. Harder to solve cleanly but works on small problems.

Caveats

  • About 10 of the 45 bulk papers are deterministic models that came up because of keyword matches ("scenario" or "robust" used in other contexts). Their signatures mark all uncertainty rows as absent / N-A. The aggregate counts above include them, but the rankings are not affected.
  • Distributionally-robust and risk-measure methods are under-represented (1 paper each) compared to their place in the broader operations-research literature. The search keywords favoured explicit "robust" or "stochastic" wording.
  • A sample of 50 is large enough for rankings but not for fine percentage claims. Round to 5% steps where it matters.

Source: 5-paper schema-derivation read on the citation-ready core (TRE 2025, TRB 2019, COR 2018, CIE 2020, TRC 2024) plus 45-paper bulk feature-signature extraction. Per-paper signatures and the full frequency CSV are in the project repository.
