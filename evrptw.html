Deterministic E-VRPTW — Literature Audit

Subject: deterministic electric vehicle routing with time windows  ·  Audit method: 4-paper foundation review (Schneider 2014, Pelletier 2016, Kucukoglu 2021, Erdelić 2019) + 40-paper implementation-corpus feature-signature audit.  ·  ← back to index
Bottom line. The audit covers 32 features across the 40 papers. The planned baseline includes every feature that appears in at least 25% of papers, and leaves out every feature that appears in 20% or fewer. It also adds one feature that no paper in the corpus uses: on-vehicle solar harvesting.

Field-consensus matrix

The table below lists every audited feature. For each one: a short description, whether the planned baseline includes it, how often it appears across the 40 papers, and the strongest references supporting the choice.
