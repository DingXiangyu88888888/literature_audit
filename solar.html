VIPV Physics and Solar-Irradiance Uncertainty — Literature Audit

Subject: vehicle-integrated photovoltaic (VIPV) generation modelling and solar-irradiance uncertainty for transportation applications.  ·  Audit method: 6-paper schema-derivation read + 14-paper bulk feature-signature extraction; 28 axes across 8 clusters; the sample is weighted toward VIPV-direct works.  ·  ← back to index
Bottom line. The audit covers 20 papers. On-vehicle photovoltaics sit between two separate research communities: VIPV physics on one side, solar-irradiance forecasting on the other. Three different ways to model VIPV irradiance compete in the physics literature. Solar forecasting is its own subfield, with well-known probabilistic benchmarks.

Spotlight works

PaperMethodological position
Araki, Ota, Yamaguchi 2020 — Measurement and modelling of 3D solar irradiance for VIPV (Appl. Sci.) [Araki2020] The most accurate approach. Uses a five-pyranometer array on a real car plus a Monte-Carlo correction for curved roofs. The downside is that real fleets do not carry this kind of sensor array.
Smitheram & Gatavi 2025 — Feasibility analysis and optimisation of VIPV systems (World Electric Veh. J.) [Smitheram2025] The most practical approach. A Simulink simulation of a Tesla Model 3 in Melbourne with a simple curvature term and a fuzzy controller for charging. Easy to use but loses direction information.
Bunme et al. 2026 — GIS-based solar-irradiance estimation in VIPV (Renew. Energy) [Bunme2026] The middle ground. Combines GIS shading data and Himawari-8/9 satellite irradiance. Validated on a Toyota HiAce in Fukushima with 3–7% mean absolute error under clear skies. Needs region-specific spatial data.
Zhang, Luo, Rashidi 2024–2025 — Three VIPV-WCV routing papers (IEEE Trans. Transp. Electrification; IEEE Trans. Intell. Transp. Syst.; IEEE ICM) [LZ-RP; LZ-MO; LZ-R0] The closest direct precedents combining VIPV generation with vehicle routing. All three use the ASHRAE clear-sky model with isotropic-diffuse transposition and a per-road-segment shading factor. Deterministic.
van der Meer, Munkhammar, Widén 2021 — A benchmark for multivariate probabilistic solar-irradiance forecasts (Sol. Energy) [Meer2021] MuPEn, a benchmark for multivariate probabilistic forecasts of GHI trajectories. It does not need copula models, which makes it transferable to road-segment-level forecasting. Evaluated at SURFRAD and Oahu using CRPS and energy/variogram scores.

Caveats

  • N = 20 is small but covers the main method types: 8 VIPV-direct papers, 4 probabilistic forecasting papers, and 3 direct VIPV-routing precedents.
  • The sample is weighted toward VIPV-direct works. The bulk corpus has about 16% VIPV-direct papers, so the VIPV percentages here over-represent the wider field by roughly 2.5×.
  • The 95% "no decomposition" count includes papers that use GHI, DNI, and DHI as separate measured inputs (i.e. papers that skip the step rather than fail at it). The narrower point is that papers which derive DNI and DHI from GHI alone are rare in this corpus.

Source: 6-paper schema-derivation read on the citation-ready core (Araki 2020, Smitheram 2025, Bunme 2026, Alfadda 2018, Meer 2021, Caldas 2019) plus 14-paper bulk feature-signature extraction (including six open-access auto-downloads, five user-fetched VIPV-physics priority papers, and three direct-precedent Lanyi Zhang VIPV-WCV papers). Per-paper signatures and the full frequency CSV are in the project repository.
