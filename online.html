Online and Rolling-Horizon Dispatch — Literature Audit

Subject: dynamic / online vehicle routing where new information arrives during the working day.  ·  Audit method: 5-paper schema-derivation read + 4-paper bulk feature-signature extraction (N = 9); categories follow Pillac et al.'s eleven classification criteria.  ·  ← back to index
Bottom line. The audit covers 9 papers. Most papers (64%) follow the same pattern: freeze what is already done and replan the rest. The decision approach is split evenly between rolling-horizon offline replanning and event-driven re-optimisation. The papers test their methods almost entirely with synthetic event simulation rather than against real operational data.

Part C) [Hof2023] Models charging-station occupancy as uncertain and uses a Markov decision process with neural networks to predict waiting times. Fast to run online but needs long offline training.
Lan & Behnamian 2022 — Real-time scheduling with flexible time windows (Swarm Evol. Comput.) [Lan2022] A rolling-horizon approach with 15 fixed time slices and a particle-swarm solver. Practical but does not look ahead.

Boundary note: what counts as "online dispatch"?

Florio et al. 2022 is a borderline case. The plan is computed offline and never re-optimised, but small recourse decisions (preventive restocking, swapping adjacent customers) happen during the route. The Pillac taxonomy calls this static-stochastic rather than dynamic. The working definition used here is simple: a paper counts as online dispatch only if at least one decision is re-computed during the day, in response to new information that arrives after the start of the shift.

Caveats

  • N = 9 is small. The percentages above are simple fractions of 9. The Pillac 2013 survey covers 117 papers and is a useful broader reference.
  • The corpus was limited to papers with full-text PDFs available. A larger corpus (N ≥ 30) would give stronger rankings.
  • One online VRPTW chapter was originally tagged for a different subfield review and surfaced here through keyword match. Its signature confirms it belongs in this audit.
